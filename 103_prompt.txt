============================================================
3. IMPORTANT ML WARNING: TEMPORAL DATA LEAKAGE
============================================================

This is one of the most important requirements.

NEVER train a model using a project's final revised cost to predict whether that same project will eventually experience cost overrun.

NEVER use actual completion date to predict a delay that happened before completion.

NEVER use future observations.

The model must simulate:

"At month M, what could we have predicted using ONLY information available by month M?"

Example:

Project:
P

Snapshot:
January 2024

Available:
- original cost
- expenditure as of January 2024
- physical progress as of January 2024
- original target date
- revised target date ONLY if it had already been revised by January 2024
- sector
- ministry
- agency
- project age
- other fields available by January 2024

Do NOT use:
- revised cost that appeared in 2025
- actual completion date from 2026
- future physical progress
- future expenditure
- future revised dates

The training pipeline must be stage-aware and time-aware.
